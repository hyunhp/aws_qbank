@ AIB-C01 D1 T1.1 applied use-case
S: A product team says its churn model outputs 'a probability of 0.82' for a customer. How should account managers interpret this?
A: The model estimates a high likelihood of churn, not a certainty, so it should guide priority rather than decide alone
B: The customer will definitely leave within 82 days unless the account manager offers a discount immediately
C: The model is 82% finished with its training and will produce final answers once training has completed
D: Exactly 82% of the customer's active contracts will be cancelled, and the remaining 18% will be renewed automatically
K: A
W: Model outputs are often probabilities. A score of 0.82 indicates high estimated risk, which should prioritize action, not replace judgment.
X: B=A probability is not a certainty or a time frame. | C=The score is a prediction, not training progress. | D=The number is a likelihood for the customer, not a split of contracts.
T: Treat model scores as probabilities that guide decisions, not certainties.

A: To test the model on unseen data and estimate how it will perform on real future cases
B: To save storage costs, since keeping all of the data in training would require a larger bucket
C: To reduce the time needed for training, since smaller datasets are always more accurate
D: To keep sensitive records out of the model, since held-back data is automatically encrypted

@ AIB-C01 D1 T1.3 advanced use-case

A: Have the assistant call a rates service at question time and use the returned values in its answer
B: Fine-tune the model every day on the latest exchange rates so it memorizes the current values
C: Include last year's exchange rates in the system prompt, since rates change only slightly over time
D: Ask the model to estimate current rates from its training data, since models know recent market data
K: A

@ AIB-C01 D1 T1.1 applied use-case

B: The model's software license expired in 2023, which gradually reduces its accuracy each month
C: Models always lose exactly one percent of accuracy per month regardless of changes in the data

@ AIB-C01 D1 T1.2 advanced tradeoff

A: Use AI as decision support that flags findings for radiologists, who make the final diagnosis
B: Let AI make final diagnoses automatically, since models never tire and are always more accurate
C: Avoid AI entirely, since any tool that can make mistakes should never be used in healthcare
D: Show radiologists only the scans the AI marks as normal, so they can focus on routine cases
