@ AIB-C01 D2 T2.1 applied use-case
S: A company's AI program has many small experiments but none reach production. Which strategic change helps most?
A: Focus on fewer use cases with clear owners, success metrics, and a path to production
B: Launch even more experiments so that at least one eventually succeeds by chance alone
C: Stop all experiments and wait for AI technology to become more mature over several years
D: Let the data science team choose projects purely based on which techniques interest them most
K: A
W: Concentrating on fewer, well-owned use cases with production plans converts experiments into value.
X: B=More experiments without focus repeats the problem. | C=Waiting forgoes learning and value. | D=Technique-driven choices often lack business ownership.
T: Focus on fewer, owned use cases with a path to production.

@ AIB-C01 D2 T2.2 advanced use-case

A: Measure time per task before and after on a sample, then multiply by task volume and loaded labor cost
