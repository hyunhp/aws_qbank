@ AIF-C01 D1 T1.1 advanced use-case
S: A fraud model flags 100 transactions, of which 80 are actually fraudulent. Which metric does 80% represent?
A: Precision
B: Recall
C: Specificity
D: R-squared
K: A
W: Precision is the share of flagged cases that are truly positive: 80 of 100.
X: B=Recall is the share of actual fraud that was caught. | C=Specificity concerns correctly identified negatives. | D=R-squared is a regression metric.
T: Precision = correct among flagged; recall = caught among actual.
