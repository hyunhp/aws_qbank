@ AIF-C01 D4 T4.1 applied use-case
S: A company fine-tunes a model on customer chat logs. Which responsible AI step should happen before training?
A: Remove or mask personal data the model should not learn
B: Increase the logs' volume by duplicating every record
C: Keep only chats that received the most positive ratings
D: Translate every chat into a single language before use
K: A
W: Removing or masking personal data prevents the model from memorizing and later exposing it.
X: B=Duplicates distort training. | C=Keeping only positive chats can bias the model. | D=Translation may be unnecessary and does not address privacy.
T: Remove personal data before fine-tuning.

@ AIF-C01 D4 T4.2 applied use-case
S: A company wants users to understand the limits of its AI assistant. Which practice supports this?
A: Explain in the interface what the assistant can and cannot do
B: Hide the assistant's limitations to encourage more usage
C: Present every answer as verified fact from the company
D: Remove the option for users to report incorrect answers
K: A
W: Clear communication of capabilities and limits helps users calibrate trust and use the assistant appropriately.
X: B=Hiding limits leads to misuse. | C=Presenting outputs as verified misleads users. | D=Reporting options enable improvement.
T: Communicate AI capabilities and limits to users.

@ AIF-C01 D4 T4.1 advanced use-case
S: A company deploys a model that recommends job ads. Which TWO practices help ensure fair outcomes over time? (Select TWO)
A: Monitor who is shown ads across demographic groups
B: Retest for bias when data or the model changes
C: Stop monitoring once the model passes launch tests
D: Optimize only for click-through rate with no checks
E: Remove group data entirely so fairness cannot be measured
K: A,B
W: Ongoing monitoring by group and retesting after changes catch unfair outcomes that emerge over time.
X: C=Fairness can drift after launch. | D=Optimizing clicks alone can amplify bias. | E=Without group data, fairness cannot be assessed.
T: Monitor and retest fairness continuously.
V: Amazon SageMaker Clarify

@ AIF-C01 D2 T2.2 applied use-case
S: A customer support team wants to use a foundation model to draft replies faster. Which approach balances speed and quality?
A: Generate drafts that agents review and edit before sending
B: Send every generated reply automatically without review
C: Use the model only for replies longer than ten paragraphs
D: Ask customers to rate each draft before agents see them
K: A
W: Human-in-the-loop drafting speeds up work while agents ensure accuracy and tone.
X: B=Unreviewed replies risk errors reaching customers. | C=Length is not the right criterion. | D=Customers should not review internal drafts.
T: Use GenAI drafts with human review for customer communication.
V: Amazon Q
