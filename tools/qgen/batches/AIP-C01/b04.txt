@ AIP-C01 D1 T1.2 applied use-case

@ AIP-C01 D1 T1.4 advanced use-case
S: A vector search index with 50 million vectors has slow queries. The team accepts slightly less exact results for much faster search. Which index approach fits?
A: An approximate nearest neighbor index such as HNSW
B: An exact brute-force scan over every vector per query
C: A relational B-tree index on the embedding column
D: A full-text inverted index built on the chunk IDs
K: A
W: ANN indexes such as HNSW trade a small amount of accuracy for large speed gains at scale.
X: B=Brute force is slow at 50 million vectors. | C=B-trees do not support vector similarity. | D=Inverted indexes on IDs do not help similarity search.
T: Scale vector search with ANN indexes such as HNSW.
