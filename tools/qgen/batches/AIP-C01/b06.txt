@ AIP-C01 D3 T3.1 applied use-case
S: A customer chatbot must refuse to discuss competitors' products regardless of how users phrase the request. Which guardrail policy fits?
A: A denied topic describing competitor product discussions
B: A word filter listing today's competitor product names
C: A content filter set to high for the violence category
D: A contextual grounding check with a strict threshold
K: A
W: Denied topics block a subject area described in natural language, however it is phrased.
X: B=Word lists miss paraphrases and new names. | C=Violence filters target a different harm. | D=Grounding checks target unsupported claims.
T: Block whole subjects with denied topics.
