W: A custom, representative dataset with paired prompts and slice-level comparison tests the actual workflow and users. Bedrock model evaluation supports custom prompt datasets with automatic metrics, LLM-as-a-judge and human review.
X: B=Toxicity only: toxicity is only one aspect. A low score says nothing about differences in helpfulness or outcomes between groups.
C=English general knowledge: it ignores Spanish speakers and the billing-dispute domain, so it cannot reveal gaps that affect them.
D=Self-generated tests and self-judging: the same model creates and grades the cases, which can miss its own blind spots, and no human sampling validates the judge.
T: For fairness evidence, use a representative custom dataset with paired prompts and per-slice comparison, not only generic public benchmarks.
