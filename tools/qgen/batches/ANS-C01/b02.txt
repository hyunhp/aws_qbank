@ ANS-C01 D2 T2.1 applied use-case
S: A company sets up a Site-to-Site VPN with dynamic routing. What must the customer gateway device support?
A: IPsec tunnels and BGP sessions over both tunnels
B: GRE tunnels only, without any IPsec encryption support
C: MACsec encryption on a dedicated fiber cross-connect
