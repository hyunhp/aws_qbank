@ DOP-C02 D3 T3.1 applied use-case
S: A stateless web tier must tolerate the loss of an Availability Zone with no manual action. Which configuration fits?
A: An Auto Scaling group spanning three AZs behind an ALB with ELB health checks
B: A fixed pair of instances in one AZ, with an Elastic IP address attached to the primary

W: Multi-AZ Auto Scaling with ELB health checks replaces failed capacity automatically.
