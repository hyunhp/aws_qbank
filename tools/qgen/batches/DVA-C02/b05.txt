@ DVA-C02 D1 T1.1 applied use-case
S: A web app must let users download private S3 files for 10 minutes without making the bucket public. Which approach fits?
A: Generate presigned GET URLs with a 10-minute expiration
B: Make the objects public and delete them after 10 minutes
C: Share the bucket owner's credentials with each user briefly
D: Enable Requester Pays so users pay for their own downloads
K: A
W: Presigned URLs grant time-limited access to private objects.

@ DVA-C02 D1 T1.3 advanced use-case
