@ MLA-C01 D1 T1.1 applied use-case
S: A training job reads a 2 TB dataset of many small files from S3 and spends most of its time waiting for data to download before training starts. Which input mode reduces startup time with minimal changes?
A: FastFile mode, which streams S3 objects on demand
B: File mode with a larger EBS volume on the instance
C: Copying the data to the notebook instance first
D: Splitting the job into 2,000 separate training jobs

@ MLA-C01 D1 T1.2 applied use-case
