# SAA-C03 batch 04: Domain 4 (cost-optimized architectures)
@ SAA-C03 D4 T4.1 applied use-case
S: Log files in S3 are accessed often for 30 days, rarely for a year, and then must be kept for seven years for compliance. Which approach minimizes cost?
A: Lifecycle rules moving objects to Standard-IA, then Glacier Deep Archive
B: Keep all objects in S3 Standard for the entire seven years
C: Store the logs on large EBS volumes attached to a dedicated logging instance
D: Move objects to Glacier Deep Archive on the first day
K: A
W: Lifecycle transitions match storage class to access patterns, cutting cost over time.
X: B=Standard is costly for rarely accessed data. | C=EBS is costlier and not durable object storage. | D=Deep Archive on day one makes frequent 30-day access slow and costly.
T: Match storage classes to access patterns with lifecycle rules.
V: Amazon S3
