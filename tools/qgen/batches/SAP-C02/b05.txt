@ SAP-C02 D3 T3.1 advanced use-case
S: An application team receives hundreds of CloudWatch alarm emails daily, most of them for transient spikes, and misses real incidents. How should monitoring be improved?
A: Use composite alarms and anomaly detection, and route actionable alarms to Incident Manager
B: Lower every alarm threshold so that more alarms fire and nothing is missed by the team
C: Delete all alarms and rely on customers to report problems through the support desk
D: Forward every alarm email to a larger distribution list so more people see each one
K: A
W: Composite alarms and anomaly detection reduce noise, and routing only actionable alarms to incident response improves focus.
X: B=More alarms increase noise. | C=Customer reports come after impact. | D=Wider distribution does not reduce noise.
T: Reduce alert fatigue with composite and anomaly alarms.
V: Amazon CloudWatch, AWS Systems Manager

@ SAP-C02 D3 T3.3 advanced use-case
